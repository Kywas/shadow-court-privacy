Privacy Policy — Shadow Court

Last updated: November 2, 2026

App: Shadow Court (com.coreforge.darkroguelite)

Contact: shadow-court@mail.ru

Summary

Shadow Court has no accounts, no advertising and no purchases. The developer does not collect your personal information and does not receive your gameplay data.

Data stored on your device

Your progress and settings (Soul Shards, permanent upgrades, unlocked relics, records, language, volume, vibration preference) are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them. Android's own device backup may copy them if you have it turned on; that is controlled by your device settings.

Game engine

The game is built with the Unity engine. The engine's optional analytics services are turned off in this game. The app package declares the Internet permission, but no game feature uses it. Unity describes its handling of data in its privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Permissions

  • Vibration: used for feedback in play, only if enabled in Settings.
  • Internet: declared by the app package, as described above. The game plays fully offline.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date.
